# Sigma Precisions — Brand Identity

**Essence:** Precision with accountability.

**Tagline:** Precision-engineered systems and components

**Positioning:** Owner-led precision engineering — design, prototype, qualify, and manufacture for power, aerospace, and oil & gas.
